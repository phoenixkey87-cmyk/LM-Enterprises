Just a Quick Nap

Private, pay-by-the-hour nap pods inside airport terminals. Launching at St. Louis Lambert International Airport, Terminal 2, then rolling out across the country.

Concept stage Seeking $800K seed STL Terminal 2 pilot

Rest between flights

Early departures, long connections, delays, and red-eyes. Airport seating was not built for sleep.

⏰

Book in seconds

Scan a code or use the kiosk, pick 30 minutes to a few hours, and get a wake-up alert before your gate time.

🛌

Private and quiet

Lie-flat bed, lockable door, sound dampening, dimmable light, and a power outlet. One accessible pod in every location.

✨

Cleaned every time

Every pod is reset and sanitized between guests, with cleaning logs. Stay past security so your boarding pass stays valid.

Our position

  • $30 per hour list price, below private suites
  • 30-minute sessions for the "just a quick nap" traveler
  • Monthly pass for frequent flyers and crew
  • Local St. Louis brand and operations

Who we serve

  • Connecting and layover travelers
  • Early-morning departures
  • International and jet-lagged arrivals (British Airways flies from Terminal 2)
  • Delayed and stranded passengers

Launch site: STL Terminal 2

A 16-pod pilot with a defined window

The plan

  • 16 pods, post-security, open about 18 hours a day
  • Opening target: January 2028, subject to airport agreement
  • Movable, modular pods with minimal permanent build-out
  • Pods redeployed to the new terminal or a second airport

What investors should know

Airport officials plan to close Terminals 1 and 2 in November 2031, and Southwest is scheduled to move to the new terminal in March 2031. Dates are tentative. Terminal 2 alone gives about 39 months of operation. On our base case it does not fully repay the investment unless the pods are redeployed or resold. The plan is built around that.
