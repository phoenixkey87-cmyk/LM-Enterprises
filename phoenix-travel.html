Planning Fee Policy

Planning fees compensate Phoenix Travel for professional research, itinerary development, travel recommendations, coordination, and planning time. Planning fees are separate from the cost of airfare, accommodations, transportation, excursions, and other travel purchases.

Planning fees are due before custom planning begins and are non-refundable once research or itinerary development has started.

Travel planning fees may still apply when Phoenix Travel books accommodations, cruises, resorts, or other travel services on the client's behalf.

Final pricing may vary depending on destination, number of travelers, length of trip, complexity, and requested services.

Family & Friends Policy

Do you offer family and friends discounts?

Occasional discounts or courtesy pricing may be offered at Phoenix Travel's discretion. Family and friend relationships do not automatically waive professional planning fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there a planning fee?

The planning fee covers professional research, itinerary development, destination expertise, personalized recommendations, and the time spent creating a customized trip tailored to your preferences and needs.

Do I still pay a planning fee if Phoenix Travel books my trip?

Yes. Supplier commissions and professional planning fees are separate forms of compensation. Depending on the trip, a planning fee may still apply.

When is the planning fee due?

Planning fees must be paid before custom research or itinerary development begins.

Are planning fees refundable?

Planning fees are non-refundable once research or planning has started.

Can I make changes to my itinerary?

Yes. Each package includes a set number of revisions. Additional revisions may be purchased.
